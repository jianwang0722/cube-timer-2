1 · Color

Neutral graphite ramp carries the whole UI. WCA sticker colors are functional, never decorative: green = ready/PB/best, yellow = inspection warning, orange = +2/12s, red = DNF. The six-color strip appears in exactly two places — the cube preview and the PB moment.

2 · Typography

Three families, strict roles. Azeret Mono owns every number. IBM Plex Mono owns notation. Instrument Sans owns UI chrome. font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums everywhere a digit appears — no exceptions, no layout shift.

3 · Spacing & layout
